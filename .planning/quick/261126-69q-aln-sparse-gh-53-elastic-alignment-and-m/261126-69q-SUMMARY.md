## Algorithm (R elasdics 1.1.3 source read: align_curves.R, fit_mean.R, find_optimal_t*.R, ...)
Curves as polygons, SRV piecewise constant; duplicates removed; closed curves closed by
appending the start. Polygon-to-polygon alignment = elasdics coordinate-wise analytic
maximisation (even/odd sweeps; closed: periodic extension + best circular shift start).
Mean: alternate long-format least-squares fit (indicator / hat basis) of warped SRVs and
per-curve alignment (parallel via `iter_maybe_parallel!`), stop on relative coef change < eps,
alignment tolerance 100·eps/i, exactly as `fit_mean`. Smooth alignment: exact piecewise
integrals of ⟨q_k, p⟩_+² for the linear SRV spline + coordinate-wise bracketed 1-D search.

## Deviations from elasdics (documented in module docs)
1. Closed `align_curves` reports its distance after wrapping t_optim into [0, 1], which breaks
   monotonicity and drops the wrapped segment (2.339 instead of 1.369 on its own example);
   we report the optimal pre-wrap distance (elasdics' internal `find_optimal_t_discrete_closed`
   value). `param_optim` is wrapped as in elasdics.
2. Smooth mean: exact integrals + bracketed coordinate search instead of `constrOptim` on
   `integrate(rel.tol = 0.1)` — agreement within optimiser tolerance, not bit-for-bit.
3. `distances` are to the returned mean (elasdics `dist_to_mean` refers to the previous
   iteration's mean).
4. Duplicate threshold `max|x|·ε` instead of `max(x)·ε`; duplicate removal also applied for
   d = 1 without a parametrisation (elasdics skips it and would then fail on ties).
5. Rank-deficient spline fits use the SVD minimum-norm solution (R `lm` gives NA).
