# 261126-b80 — joint_gauss_model keeps amplitude–phase correlation (GH #80)

**Goal:** `joint_gauss_model` must sample the joint amplitude/phase scores from
`N(mean, Σ)` with the *full* sample covariance Σ of `[vert | c·horiz]` scores,
not a diagonal Gaussian.

**Root cause:** `joint_score_mean_var` kept only column variances and
`sample_joint_z` drew each component independently, dropping the cross-covariance.

**Fix:** `joint_score_mean_cov_factor` computes Σ and its PSD square root
`V diag(sqrt(max(λ,0)))` (nalgebra `SymmetricEigen`, robust to rank deficiency);
`sample_joint_z` draws `mean + L z` (same number of RNG draws). Equivalent in
distribution to fdasrsf/fdasrvf jfPCA sampling (independent normals in the
joint-PCA basis of the score matrix).

**Validation:** (1) factor·factorᵀ reproduces the full covariance of a
rank-deficient hand matrix; (2) the issue repro (height/peak-location linked,
data corr 0.999) — sample corr must exceed 0.8 (was ≈ 0).
