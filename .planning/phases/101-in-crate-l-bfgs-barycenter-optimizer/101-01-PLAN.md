<objective>
Replace the fixed-step MM/diagonal-Newton descent inside `soft_dtw_barycenter` with a
hand-rolled limited-memory L-BFGS local optimizer (two-loop recursion + backtracking
Armijo line search) driven by the exact soft-DTW gradient the crate already computes,
delivering requirement SDTW-01. Single-initialization path only; the public 4-arg
signature and the `SoftDtwBarycenterResult` shape are preserved byte-for-byte. Compiles
and runs in the default feature set and on `wasm32-unknown-unknown` — no `linalg`/`argmin`
gating, no new crate dependency (hand-rolled in pure Rust, `std::collections::VecDeque` +
`f64` arithmetic only).

Purpose: Close the last in-core algorithm-quality item (`SDTW-O1`) — the MM step stalls
in local minima of the non-convex soft-DTW objective; a proper quasi-Newton refiner
converges to a lower cost on curved objectives while staying bounded.

Output: An updated `fdars-core/src/metric/soft_dtw.rs` with the L-BFGS optimizer, the
dead `update_barycenter` removed, and a new `barycenter_lbfgs_tests` module covering the
five SDTW-01 behaviors from 101-VALIDATION.md plus a wasm32 compile guard.
</objective>
