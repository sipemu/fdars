<objective>
Add formal functional-linear-model inference to the Phase-20 `inference/` module: `flm_f_test` (overall-significance F-test on a fitted `FregreLmResult`), `flm_gof_test` (residual-based goodness-of-fit test), and `oneway_anova_vstat` (asymptotic one-way functional ANOVA V-statistic added alongside the existing permutation `fanova`). Closes INF-02, the final requirement of milestone v0.19.0.

Purpose: fdars can formally test the adequacy and significance of a fitted FLM and offers the asymptotic V-statistic ANOVA form — closing two of the three remaining R-parity Area-5 table-stakes gaps (with INF-01 already shipped in Phase 20).

Output: a self-contained F-distribution survival function (no new crate dependency), three crate-root re-exported `Result`-returning functions with inline correctness tests, and zero changes to any existing public signature.
</objective>
